# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

NoxaPlus SpecForge is a tool for people who want to keep building while the contract is the only thing that exists. Participants are expected to keep discussions technical, specific, and respectful, regardless of experience, background, identity, or the size of the project they are working on.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent, but be precise about technical concerns.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain the impact of a problem and, when possible, suggest a fix.
- Treat mock data as data: do not use generated names, emails, or records to make assumptions about real people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's specs, tokens, private API documents, or personal information.
- Follow the issue and pull request templates so reports stay actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues, spamming, or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.
- Submitting content that is illegal or that violates GitHub's terms.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other project space. It also applies when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com**. Include links or screenshots when possible. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or block accounts, and will explain the decision when it is safe to do so.

## Attribution

This policy is written for SpecForge and is not a verbatim copy of a template. It is informed by common open-source community guidelines, including the Contributor Covenant.
